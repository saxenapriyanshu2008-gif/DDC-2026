The job

01Mission

The Getaway Laptop

You and your partner sit back to back with one laptop. One player codes while a 15 second timer runs. When it hits zero, the laptop switches hands, finished or not. Keep switching until every problem is solved.

  • No talking, signals or notes to your partner
  • Break the rule and you take a sour shot
  • Fastest crew to solve every problem wins the mission
Round 1
02Mission

The Stakeout

You get a few minutes with a secret file you have never seen. Read it, lock it in your head, then hand it back. After that comes the questioning, and the only thing you have is what you remember.

  • Unseen passage, short reading time
  • No notes, no phones
  • Most correct answers wins the mission
Round 2
03Mission

Classified

Nobody gets the brief for this one. Only the crews still standing find out what the final job is, and they find out on the day.
